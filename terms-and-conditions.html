EGR Harvest + Beer Festival — Volunteer SMS Program

Effective Date: May 1, 2026

These Terms and Conditions govern participation in the EGR Harvest + Beer Festival Volunteer SMS Notification Program ("Program") operated by the EGR Harvest + Beer Festival ("we," "us," or "our"), located in East Grand Rapids, Michigan.

1. Program Name & Description

Program Name: EGR Harvest + Beer Festival Volunteer SMS Notifications

This program sends automated SMS text messages to registered volunteers of the EGR Harvest + Beer Festival. Messages include shift reminders, schedule confirmations, assignment updates, and other logistical information directly related to your volunteer participation at the festival.

2. How to Enroll

You enroll in this SMS program by providing your mobile phone number when registering as a volunteer at volunteers.egrharvestfest.com. By submitting your phone number and opting-in for text reminders, you consent to receive automated text messages from the EGR Harvest + Beer Festival.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on your volunteer schedule and proximity to the festival. You may receive up to 5–10 messages per festival season, including shift confirmation, reminders as your shift approaches, and any schedule changes.

4. Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Standard SMS and data rates charged by your mobile carrier apply to all messages sent and received through this program. EGR Harvest + Beer Festival does not charge any additional fees for this service.

5. Opt-Out Instructions

To stop receiving SMS messages at any time, reply STOP to any message from this program. You will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent to your number.

For help or more information, reply HELP to any message or contact us at info@egrharvestfest.com.

6. Support Contact Information

For questions or assistance with this program, contact us at:

  • Website: www.egrharvestfest.com
  • SMS: Reply HELP to any program message

7. Privacy

Your personal information, including your mobile phone number, will not be shared with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes. For full details on how we collect, use, and protect your information, please review our Privacy Policy.

8. Eligibility

This program is available to registered volunteers of the EGR Harvest + Beer Festival who have provided a valid US mobile phone number. Participants must be 18 years of age or older to register as a volunteer.

We reserve the right to modify these Terms and Conditions at any time. Updated terms will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued participation in the program following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
