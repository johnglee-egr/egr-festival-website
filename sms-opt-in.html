Volunteer SMS Opt-In

EGR Harvest Festival — Volunteer Shift Reminder Program

How Volunteers Opt In

Volunteers opt in on our volunteer registration page at volunteers.egrharvestfest.com/volunteer. After entering their name and mobile phone number, the volunteer checks a box that is unchecked by default. Consent is never pre-selected, and it is not a condition of volunteering — volunteers who leave the box unchecked receive email only.

This is the exact checkbox and wording shown on the registration form:

Yes, text me shift reminders. I agree to receive recurring automated SMS text messages from EGR Harvest Fest about my volunteer shifts at the number provided. Message frequency varies (about 5–10 messages per festival season). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. See our Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Consent is not a condition of volunteering. Leave this unchecked and we'll email you instead.

Program Details

  • Program name: EGR Harvest Fest Volunteer SMS Notifications
  • Who opts in: people who register as volunteers for the festival
  • Where: the volunteer registration form linked above (publicly accessible, no login required to reach the form)
  • What we send: shift confirmations, shift reminders, and schedule changes for that volunteer's own assignment
  • Message frequency: varies by shift schedule — approximately 5–10 messages per festival season
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply
  • Opt out: reply STOP to any message
  • Help: reply HELP to any message

This is the only opt-in method for this campaign. There is no keyword, paper form, or verbal opt-in.

Sample Messages

EGR Harvest Fest: Hi! Reminder: you're signed up for "Pour - Shift 1" on Sat Oct 10, 12:00 PM-2:00 PM. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
EGR Harvest Fest: Your shift assignment changed to "Ice Runners - Shift 2", Sat Oct 10, 2:00 PM-4:00 PM. Reply STOP to opt out.

Privacy

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent is never shared with any third parties or affiliates. Full details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
