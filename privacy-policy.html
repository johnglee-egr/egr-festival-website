Privacy Policy

EGR Harvest + Beer Festival — Volunteer SMS Program

Effective Date: May 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the EGR Harvest + Beer Festival ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through our volunteer registration portal and SMS notification program.

1. Information We Collect

When you register as a volunteer, we collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Email address (optional)
  • Mobile phone number
  • Age verification (whether you are 21 or older, for applicable roles)
  • Volunteer role preference and shift assignments
  • Communication preference (email, SMS, or both)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • To register and manage your volunteer participation at the EGR Harvest + Beer Festival
  • To send you SMS text messages and/or emails with shift reminders, schedule confirmations, and festival updates relevant to your volunteer assignment
  • To coordinate volunteer teams and shift assignments
  • To contact you with important event logistics information

We will only send SMS messages to phone numbers you have voluntarily provided for this purpose.

3. SMS Messaging

By providing your mobile phone number and registering as a volunteer, you consent to receive automated SMS text messages from the EGR Harvest + Beer Festival regarding your volunteer shifts and related event updates. Message frequency varies based on your shift schedule. Standard message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. For help, reply HELP.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes. Your information is not used for advertising of any kind.

We may share limited information only in the following circumstances:

  • With our SMS service provider (Twilio) solely to deliver text messages on our behalf — they are contractually prohibited from using your data for any other purpose
  • As required by law or valid legal process

5. Data Retention

We retain volunteer information for the duration of the festival season and for a reasonable period afterward for recordkeeping purposes. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Our volunteer portal uses secure HTTPS connections and access controls.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
